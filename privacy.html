LS410 ECU Reader – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

English

LS410 ECU Reader is an independent diagnostic and monitoring application designed to communicate locally with compatible OBD-II/ELM327 Bluetooth adapters and supported motorcycle ECUs.

Data collection and transmission

LS410 ECU Reader does not require a user account, does not contain advertising, and does not sell personal information. The app does not transmit motorcycle diagnostic data to servers operated by the developer.

Bluetooth and diagnostic data

The app uses Bluetooth to communicate with a paired OBD-II/ELM327 adapter. It may read technical information provided by the vehicle ECU, such as diagnostic trouble codes (DTCs), live sensor values, ECU identification information and session data. This information is processed locally on the user's device.

Sharing

If the user chooses to copy, export or share a diagnostic report or other information from the app, that action is initiated and controlled by the user. The privacy practices of the selected destination app or service are governed by that third party.

Permissions

Bluetooth permissions are used only to discover, connect to and communicate with compatible paired diagnostic adapters as required by Android.

Children

The app is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party and trademark notice

LS410 ECU Reader is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official application of Royal Enfield. Product and company names may be trademarks of their respective owners.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's data practices or features change. The latest version will be published on this page.
